Getting started

What is Smart Community?

Smart Community is a local service marketplace. You search for a service, compare nearby providers, and send a clear request. Every job, message, and update stays together in your dashboard.

Do I need an account to use Smart Community?

You can browse services and try the interactive demo without an account. You need a free account to send a request, save providers, leave reviews, or manage a provider profile.

How do I send a request?

Open the service you need and choose Request. Add the date, your location, and a short description of the job, then send it. You can follow the provider's response from your dashboard.

For providers

How do I offer my services as a provider?

Create an account and choose the provider role. From your dashboard you can add a business profile, list your services with prices, set your availability, and respond to incoming requests.

Where do prices come from?

Each provider sets the price and price unit shown on their listing. Review the details before you send a request so you know what the service includes.

How do I know a provider is trustworthy?

Provider profiles show the business name, location, listed services, and ratings from customer reviews. Reading recent reviews is the best way to judge whether a provider fits your job.

Account and support

Can I update my request or my account details?

Yes. Open your dashboard to review requests, update your profile, and change your password. For an active job, use the request to keep the conversation with your provider in one place.

What should I do if something is not working?

Try the interactive demo to check the request flow without an account. If a page or action still fails, send us a note through the contact form with the request number, if you have one, and we will look into it.
